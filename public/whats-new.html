NewLatest update · Oct 2026

AI memory that survives every update

The advisor's memory of your trades used to be wiped whenever the site was updated. Now your trades live in the database, and the AI's memory is rebuilt from them automatically.

  • Automatic rebuild. When the AI service starts, it re-reads every trade from MongoDB and re-indexes it in a few seconds.
  • Self-healing. If your memory is missing when you ask the advisor something, it is rebuilt for you on the spot.
  • See it and re-sync it. The new AI memory panel shows how many trades are saved and how many the advisor can recall.
  • Ready for your Trading DNA. A new store for behavioral patterns (panic sells, FOMO buys) that the next release fills in.

NewPhase 2A · Oct 2026

Your own account with $100,000 of virtual cash

Nivesh-Path is now a real paper-trading platform: every user has a private account and a cash balance that moves with each trade.

  • Secure sign-in. Passwords are hashed and every request carries a signed token; you only ever see your own trades.
  • Virtual cash ledger. Start with $100,000. Buying spends cash, selling returns it, at the live market price.
  • Safe orders. You can't spend more than you have or sell shares you don't own, even if you click twice.
  • New Portfolio page. Cash, holdings, allocation, profit and loss and recent activity in one place.

Phase 1 · Oct 2026

AI Portfolio Advisor, powered by Claude

Get a plain-English read on your portfolio: risk level, diversification score, strengths, risks and next steps.

  • Grounded in your history. The advisor looks up your most relevant past trades before answering and shows you which ones it used.
  • Numbers you can trust. Profit, weights and concentration are calculated in code, never guessed by the AI.
  • Always answers. If the AI is unavailable, a rule-based analysis takes over so the page never breaks.

Phase 1 · Oct 2026

A redesigned, faster site

A new design system across every page, plus a rebuilt trading flow.

  • One look everywhere. New typography, cards, animations, a mobile menu and friendly notifications instead of pop-ups.
  • Live quotes, cached. Stock prices are fetched on the server and cached, so pages load fast and API keys never reach your browser.
  • Fixed trading. Every company page now records the right ticker at the live price.
